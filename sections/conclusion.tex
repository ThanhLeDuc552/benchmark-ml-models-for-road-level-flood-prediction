\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper benchmarked six machine learning models from five families for road-level flood prediction and flood-aware routing during the 2022 Da Nang flood, using labels from a hydraulic simulation. Under spatial cross-validation, the five nonlinear models performed similarly (AUC-PR 0.796--0.840, highest for random forest), and random cross-validation overstated AUC-ROC by up to 0.094. As routing costs, the MLP's probabilities reduced flooded distance by 41.2\% for a 16.5\% increase in travel time, so the best classifier was not the best router at a fixed risk weight, and even an oracle could not avoid flooding on 94.7\% of trips. Terrain, led by depression depth, drove the predictions of every model.

Future work will validate the labels against observed flood reports, add further events, tune hyperparameters over several seeds, and add risk costs to reinforcement-learning routing~\cite{le2026}. The risk maps could also provide prior flood probabilities for placing road sensors or cameras in Da Nang, following~\cite{pan2026}.
